TOPIC. This is a BROAD security-and-sovereignty topic screen, not a test that propaganda or hybrid warfare occurred. related means substantive discussion of national/country sovereignty, territorial integrity, statehood, strategic autonomy, national security, armed conflict, military capability/readiness/deterrence, or coercive security activity such as cyber operations, sabotage, information operations, economic coercion or political interference. It includes defensive preparation and security threats outside declared war. Ordinary politics, criticism of government, tourism, economic hardship, military-looking emojis, geography without substantive sovereignty/security discussion, sports rivalry, and national pride alone are NOT related. A sovereignty explainer can be related even with no attack or military preparation. Domains sovereignty, military_capability and national_security cover these broad topics. Never infer propaganda or a coordinated hostile operation from topic membership. A mere mention of war in an unrelated list is insufficient. Historical warfare qualifies as related but historical, never current. Fictional or speculative wars are hypothetical. Use unclear when the supplied evidence cannot resolve the topic; short text with a clear topic CAN suffice. Domains are multi-label; [] for not_related/unclear. Cybercrime is a cyber topic, but that does not establish state involvement or hybrid warfare. Misleading content is not automatically information_operations: that domain requires the material to discuss strategic influence/propaganda/disinformation activity. Distinguish evidence describing allegations from asserted reports; reported is not verified.

ROLES describe how a specific country, continent or political entity is PORTRAYED in the supplied evidence. EXECUTION (adapted from Dall’Olio & Vakratsas, Table 5; consumer-advertising categories, not a validated war-content effectiveness scale). Only annotate execution for relevance=related. Return exactly the original five categories comparative, endorsement, entertainment, imagery_visual, mnemonic_devices PLUS verbal_imagery, a separate study-specific extension (not an original paper category). Each has status, exact transcript evidence quote/source_id when present, and rationale. Outside related, all six are not_applicable. If transcript_truncated, do not infer absence from omitted material.
comparative: direct OR clearly implied contrast of countries, forces, capabilities, institutions, rights, citizenship/residency rules, daily life or systems, or superiority boasting (puffery). The contrast must be expressed in the supplied text: do not invent a comparator using background knowledge. Two country names alone are insufficient. A narration contrasting citizenship as a job with citizenship as a birthright qualifies even without naming another country; describing a citizenship rule alone without contrast does not. A security/sovereignty-related video may compare nonmilitary aspects; do not restrict this category to weapons or power. Clear implied contrasts are an explicit adaptation beyond the paper’s direct-comparison definition.
endorsement: identified expert, official, celebrity, or eyewitness/personal testimony used to lend credibility to a claim. A name mentioned in news is not enough; ordinary unnamed narration is not automatically expert endorsement. This describes the delivery device, not agreement by the publisher.
entertainment: explicit humor/comedy, dramatization, or a constructed story with beginning, development, conclusion; routine chronological reporting of events is not automatically storytelling.
imagery_visual: charts, striking imagery, rapid montage, visual effects/complexity. ALWAYS not_observable for transcript-only model input, even if text says 'look at this map'. No video frames were supplied.
mnemonic_devices: The paper includes a brand/product name repeated at least three times; our adaptation allows a clearly identified focal country or institution name repeated at least three times in narration. State the repeated name and its observed repetition in the rationale, with an exact quote. Do not equate any memorable description with a mnemonic device. Other indicators: distinctive slogan/catchphrase, repeated identifiable name (3+ actual mentions in transcript), memorable character or audio cue. Plain background [Music] captions do not establish a memorable jingle. Verbal repetition can be present when evidenced; otherwise not_observable because sound/visual identity is unobserved.
verbal_imagery: vivid verbal scene-setting that evokes a mental picture through concrete spatial, sensory or atmospheric detail. A narrated quiet square under lamps, motionless guard and illuminated palace windows qualifies from transcript evidence. A bare list of population/area statistics or generic praise does not. This is a new descriptive extension, distinct from imagery_visual; do not infer camera shots or production quality from words.
For comparative, endorsement, entertainment or verbal_imagery, use not_observed_in_transcript if no verbal evidence in the available complete transcript, NOT a claim of absence from the video. If truncated or ambiguous use not_observable. For present status quotes MUST come from transcript sections, not titles/descriptions. Never assume cinematic editing, imagery, music, charisma, emotional impact or effectiveness from the transcript. Human reviewers will supply audiovisual judgments after watching.
