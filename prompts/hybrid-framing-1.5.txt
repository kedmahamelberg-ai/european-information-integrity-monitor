ROLES describe how a specific country, continent or political entity is PORTRAYED in the supplied evidence. List separate entity-role entries, possibly several for one entity. readiness = preparing, training, strengthening defence or resilience; projecting_power = demonstrating military power, threatening, coercing or conducting an attack; under_pressure = portrayed as attacked, hacked, sabotaged, coerced or threatened. Training alone is readiness, NOT aggression. Responding to an attack may give an entity more than one role. Do not transfer one actor's role to every country mentioned. Role entity_code must be a supplied European code, EU for explicitly generic Europe/EU, or OTHER for an outside entity; preserve its actual name. Do not force a role if none is evidenced. VIEWPOINT STRUCTURE AND CONTENT REFERENCES. Establish the reference frame BEFORE classifying comments. viewpoint_structure=single_viewpoint when the video advances or reports one attributable proposition, multiple_viewpoints when it substantively presents two or more competing propositions, and unclear only when the supplied evidence cannot distinguish these. A debate, interview or panel is not automatically multiple_viewpoints: the transcript must contain distinct substantive positions. For Ceuta sovereignty, for example, "Ceuta is an integral part of Spain" and "Ceuta should not be part of Spain" are separate viewpoints, not one mixed message.

Return every distinct viewpoint with a stable lowercase viewpoint_id (viewpoint_1, viewpoint_2, ...), speaker (the named person/role responsible for that proposition when identified; otherwise narrator/unidentified), summary (one concise attributed proposition), target (person, policy, institution, action or topic that proposition evaluates/describes), speaker_sentiment (positive/negative/mixed/neutral/unclear toward THAT target), and an EXACT short transcript evidence quote. Speaker/narrator is attribution: who is responsible for the proposition. It is not ideology, truth, nationality, publisher endorsement or personality. Neutral reporting stays neutral; ambiguity stays unclear. The top-level content_reference is an overall frame retained for compatibility: for one viewpoint it mirrors that viewpoint; for a debate it neutrally summarizes the competing positions and uses "Multiple speakers" where appropriate. Never collapse incompatible positions into a single sentiment. Do not guess a viewpoint from comments.

EXECUTION (adapted from Dall’Olio & Vakratsas, Table 5; consumer-advertising categories, not a validated war-content effectiveness scale). Annotate delivery independently of Monitor relevance: related, not_related and unclear videos can all use delivery techniques. Return exactly FOUR categories: comparative, endorsement, entertainment, mnemonic_devices. Imagery/visual is omitted from this video-only study by researcher choice; do not return it. Each has status, exact transcript evidence quote/source_id when present, and rationale. Use not_applicable only when the delivery category genuinely does not apply to the video; do not use it merely because the subject is outside Monitor scope. Distinguish this from not_observed_in_transcript (the category could apply but is not evidenced in complete text) and not_observable (available evidence cannot assess it). If transcript_truncated, do not infer absence from omitted material.
comparative: direct OR clearly implied contrast of countries, forces, capabilities, institutions, rights, citizenship/residency rules, daily life or systems, or superiority boasting (puffery). The contrast must be expressed in the supplied text: do not invent a comparator using background knowledge. Two country names alone are insufficient. A narration contrasting citizenship as a job with citizenship as a birthright qualifies even without naming another country; describing a citizenship rule alone without contrast does not. A security/sovereignty-related video may compare nonmilitary aspects; do not restrict this category to weapons or power. Clear implied contrasts are an explicit adaptation beyond the paper’s direct-comparison definition.
endorsement: identified expert, official, celebrity, or eyewitness/personal testimony used to lend credibility to a claim. A name mentioned in news is not enough; ordinary unnamed narration is not automatically expert endorsement. This describes the delivery device, not agreement by the publisher.
entertainment: explicit humor/comedy, dramatization OR narrated historical storytelling. A narrator recounting past events, protagonists, conflict, actions and outcome counts as storytelling, even when factual, educational or documentary, without requiring jokes or fiction. Historical battle narration (including a narrated victory before Lepanto) is present. A date or isolated historical fact alone is insufficient. This historical-narration rule is an explicit study adaptation.
mnemonic_devices: The paper includes a brand/product name repeated at least three times; our adaptation allows a clearly identified focal country or institution name repeated at least three times in narration. State the repeated name and its observed repetition in the rationale, with an exact quote. Do not equate any memorable description with a mnemonic device. Other indicators: distinctive slogan/catchphrase, repeated identifiable name (3+ actual mentions in transcript), memorable character or audio cue. Plain background [Music] captions do not establish a memorable jingle. Verbal repetition can be present when evidenced; otherwise not_observable because sound/visual identity is unobserved.
For comparative, endorsement or entertainment, use not_observed_in_transcript if no verbal evidence in the available complete transcript, NOT a claim of absence from the video. If truncated or ambiguous use not_observable. For present status quotes MUST come from transcript sections, not titles/descriptions. Never assume cinematic editing, imagery, music, charisma, emotional impact or effectiveness from the transcript. Human reviewers will supply audiovisual judgments after watching.
