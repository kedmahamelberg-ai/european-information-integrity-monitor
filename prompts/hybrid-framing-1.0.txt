TOPIC. related means discussion of armed conflict, military capability/readiness/deterrence, or coercive security activity such as cyber operations, sabotage, information operations, economic coercion or political interference. It includes defensive preparation and security threats outside declared war. Ordinary politics, criticism of government, tourism, economic hardship, military-looking emojis, geography, sports rivalry, and national pride alone are NOT related. A mere mention of war in an unrelated list is insufficient. Historical warfare qualifies as related but historical, never current. Fictional or speculative wars are hypothetical. Use unclear when the supplied evidence cannot resolve the topic; short text with a clear topic CAN suffice. Domains are multi-label; [] for not_related/unclear. Cybercrime is a cyber topic, but that does not establish state involvement or hybrid warfare. Misleading content is not automatically information_operations: that domain requires the material to discuss strategic influence/propaganda/disinformation activity. Distinguish evidence describing allegations from asserted reports; reported is not verified.

ROLES describe how a specific country, continent or political entity is PORTRAYED in the supplied evidence. List separate entity-role entries, possibly several for one entity. readiness = preparing, training, strengthening defence or resilience; projecting_power = demonstrating military power, threatening, coercing or conducting an attack; under_pressure = portrayed as attacked, hacked, sabotaged, coerced or threatened. Training alone is readiness, NOT aggression. Responding to an attack may give an entity more than one role. Do not transfer one actor's role to every country mentioned. Role entity_code must be a supplied European code, EU for explicitly generic Europe/EU, or OTHER for an outside entity; preserve its actual name. Do not force a role if none is evidenced. No roles on not_related/unclear videos. Historical/hypothetical role framing stays in that context. An attributed accusation still counts as portrayal, NOT as established responsibility. Every role needs an EXACT short quotation, source_id and an explanation including attribution/uncertainty.

VIDEO STANCE. Extract the main evaluative proposition explicitly presented, about any topic (even unrelated to war). expressed or mixed needs a proposition, speaker/attribution, and EXACT evidence quote. Neutral reporting can have no_stance. A quoted interviewee is not automatically the publisher's view: identify the speaker and classify agreement against that explicitly attributed proposition. If several incompatible views have no dominant stance, use mixed with a faithful compound proposition; comments may support one part, oppose another. A factual news title does not by itself express approval/disapproval. unclear = evidence too thin; no_stance = no expressed evaluative position in the available material. Comments must never supply missing video stance.

COMMENTS. EXECUTION (adapted from Dall’Olio & Vakratsas, Table 5; consumer-advertising categories, not a validated war-content effectiveness scale). Only annotate execution for relevance=related. Return exactly the five categories comparative, endorsement, entertainment, imagery_visual, mnemonic_devices. Each has status, exact transcript evidence quote/source_id when present, and rationale. Outside related, all five are not_applicable. If transcript_truncated, do not infer absence from omitted material.
comparative: explicit comparison of countries, forces, capabilities, systems, or superiority boasting (puffery), not any mention of two countries.
endorsement: identified expert, official, celebrity, or eyewitness/personal testimony used to lend credibility to a claim. A name mentioned in news is not enough; ordinary unnamed narration is not automatically expert endorsement. This describes the delivery device, not agreement by the publisher.
entertainment: explicit humor/comedy, dramatization, or a constructed story with beginning, development, conclusion; routine chronological reporting of events is not automatically storytelling.
imagery_visual: charts, striking imagery, rapid montage, visual effects/complexity. ALWAYS not_observable for transcript-only model input, even if text says 'look at this map'. No video frames were supplied.
mnemonic_devices: distinctive slogan/catchphrase, repeated identifiable name (3+ actual mentions in transcript), memorable character or audio cue. Plain background [Music] captions do not establish a memorable jingle. Verbal repetition can be present when evidenced; otherwise not_observable because sound/visual identity is unobserved.
For comparative, endorsement or entertainment, use not_observed_in_transcript if no verbal evidence in the available complete transcript, NOT a claim of absence from the video. If truncated or ambiguous use not_observable. For present status quotes MUST come from transcript sections, not titles/descriptions. Never assume cinematic editing, imagery, music, charisma, emotional impact or effectiveness from the transcript. Human reviewers will supply audiovisual judgments after watching.
