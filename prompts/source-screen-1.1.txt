A COMPLETE OPINION ABOUT A POLITICAL TARGET IS USABLE EVIDENCE. It needs no facts, explanation, specific policy or transcript. Statements about liking, disliking, understanding, trusting or admiring a government, council, political party or its supporters qualify. First-person political feelings are public-affairs discourse. Neutral facts and historical accounts also qualify.

Choose:
scored = at least one complete political/social claim, description OR opinion is present. Any complete statement about government performance or political actors/supporters qualifies. A title can contain the whole statement. Missing transcript does not invalidate the supplied text.
out_of_scope = substantive content is only tourism, art, sport, gaming, entertainment or geography trivia, without a political/social proposition.
insufficient_evidence = no complete meaningful statement in any section; only empty text, hashtags, a topic heading or a teaser.

Examples: "I admire the mayor" is scored. "I dislike opposition voters" is scored. "Those party supporters think differently from me" is scored. "The council failed to repair the road" is scored. "Parliament approved the treaty in 1939" is scored. "Parliament" alone is insufficient_evidence. "Touring marble sculptures and buying museum tickets" is out_of_scope.
