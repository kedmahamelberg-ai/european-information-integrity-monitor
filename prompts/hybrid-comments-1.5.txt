1. response_focus: main_message = evaluation of a viewpoint's claim/policy/narrative; video_target = evaluation of the person/institution/action targeted by that viewpoint; speaker = ONLY a speaker's personality, skill, credibility or delivery; presentation = ONLY images, music, editing, channel or production; mixed = addresses more than one of these; unrelated = off topic; unclear = cannot tell. 2. viewpoint_ids identifies the exact attributed viewpoint or viewpoints the comment substantively addresses. Use only IDs supplied in viewpoints. supports = endorses that viewpoint proposition; opposes = rejects/disputes that viewpoint; mixed = accepts and rejects material parts or addresses competing viewpoints in different directions; no_position = relevant but neither inferable; unrelated = off topic; unclear = side or meaning cannot be resolved. supports/opposes/mixed requires at least one viewpoint_id and a stance_target naming the precise proposition. Empty viewpoint_ids and target are allowed only for no_position/unrelated/unclear. Speaker/presentation-only focus cannot be supports/opposes/mixed. Do not compute alignment merely by multiplying sentiment signs.
3. sentiment is positive/negative/mixed/neutral/unclear toward sentiment_target, the specifically named person, policy, event, speaker skill or production element actually evaluated. Use a noun phrase for sentiment_target, never simply 'positive' or 'negative'. If several targets receive opposite evaluations use mixed and name each with its direction. Negative sentiment can SUPPORT a negative main message. Supporting a narrator does not mean supporting the target they criticize.

EMOJI INTERPRETATION. Positive or celebratory reactions such as applause, hearts, celebration, approval, prayerful affirmation, or equivalent praise SUPPORT an identifiable viewpoint when the video context makes that proposition the clear referent. Treat those reactions as audience endorsement even when they contain no words. In a debate with competing viewpoints, emoji alone usually cannot identify a side: use unclear with no viewpoint_id unless accompanying words, a named actor, reply context supplied in the evidence, or another explicit cue resolves the referent. Laughter can support satire or mock a speaker/target, so resolve its direction from supplied evidence. Flags alone show identity or affinity and do not establish support for a policy claim. Keep alignment separate from sentiment.
