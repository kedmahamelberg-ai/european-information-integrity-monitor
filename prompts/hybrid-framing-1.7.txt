TOPICS AND SCOPE. This study includes security, sovereignty AND the essential resources and systems on which a country depends. related includes meaningful discussion of water availability/quality/reserves, food supply/agriculture, energy security/dependence, critical resources, infrastructure, health/biosecurity, climate risks and civil protection, economic resilience/strategic dependencies, technology supply chains, military/war/threats, statehood/borders/rights, cyber, influence operations, diplomacy and political institutions when linked to sovereignty or collective security. A national groundwater warning about unsafe drinking water and finite reserves IS related with topic water, even with no military actor or explicit security terminology. Natural flood protection affecting national habitability also qualifies with water/civil_protection. Do NOT require an explicit adversary, war or invasion. Ordinary lifestyle, recipes, tourism, isolated legal trivia or generic political insults without a substantive collective resource/security/sovereignty issue remain not_related. A quirky baby-name rule or snow-shovel requirement is not collective security merely because it names a government, country or law; do not add water, borders or political-institutions topics from incidental words. A substantive citizenship or statehood rule can still be related. A mixed-topic source is related when the supplied evidence contains a substantive security, sovereignty, conflict, strategic-resource or collective-resilience proposition, even if its title or surrounding material is religious, cultural, historical or personal. An isolated household or single-building maintenance complaint is not collective security by itself; require an evidenced connection to public or critical infrastructure, civil protection, systemic exposure, state capacity or wider resilience before coding it related. Supply constraints qualify without alleging that a country plans war. Never infer propaganda, intention to invade, coercion, responsibility or a coordinated hybrid operation merely from scarcity or topic membership. Use unclear for genuinely insufficient evidence. Topics are multi-label: choose the most specific directly supported topics from the schema; [] outside related. Avoid adding every vaguely associated topic. 'Water' does not automatically require sovereignty_borders, armed_conflict or military_defence. evidence_status distinguishes reports, allegations and hypotheticals; reported is not verified. Do NOT return temporal context or security domains.

ROLES describe how a specific country, continent or political entity is PORTRAYED in the supplied evidence. List separate entity-role entries, possibly several for one entity. readiness = preparing, training, strengthening defence or resilience; projecting_power = demonstrating military power, threatening, coercing or conducting an attack; under_pressure = portrayed as attacked, hacked, sabotaged, coerced or threatened. Training alone is readiness, NOT aggression. Responding to an attack may give an entity more than one role. Do not transfer one actor's role to every country mentioned. Role entity_code must be a supplied European code, EU for explicitly generic Europe/EU, or OTHER for an outside entity; preserve its actual name. Do not force a role if none is evidenced. No roles on not_related/unclear videos. If referring to a past/hypothetical actor, preserve that qualification in entity naming/rationale; no separate temporal label. An attributed accusation still counts as portrayal, NOT as established responsibility. Every role needs an EXACT short quotation, source_id and an explanation including attribution/uncertainty.

VIEWPOINT STRUCTURE AND CONTENT REFERENCES. Establish the reference frame BEFORE classifying comments. viewpoint_structure=single_viewpoint when the video advances or reports one attributable proposition, multiple_viewpoints when it substantively presents two or more competing propositions, and unclear only when the supplied evidence cannot distinguish these. A debate, interview or panel is not automatically multiple_viewpoints: the transcript must contain distinct substantive positions. For Ceuta sovereignty, for example, "Ceuta is an integral part of Spain" and "Ceuta should not be part of Spain" are separate viewpoints, not one mixed message.

Use this order: (1) split the content into distinct substantive propositions; (2) attribute each proposition; (3) identify each proposition's target; (4) code the responsible speaker's sentiment toward that target. Do not assign a single overall sentiment before separating competing propositions.

Return every distinct viewpoint with a stable lowercase viewpoint_id (viewpoint_1, viewpoint_2, ...), speaker (the named person/role responsible for that proposition when identified; otherwise narrator/unidentified), summary (one concise attributed proposition), target (person, policy, institution, action or topic that proposition evaluates/describes), speaker_sentiment (positive/negative/mixed/neutral/unclear toward THAT target), and an EXACT short transcript evidence quote. Speaker/narrator is attribution: who is responsible for the proposition. It is not ideology, truth, nationality, publisher endorsement or personality. Use a named speaker only when the supplied evidence identifies that person as responsible for the words or proposition. A person merely discussed, the publisher/channel, the audio language, or a presumed nationality is not the speaker. In multiple-viewpoint content, attribute each proposition separately; do not assign one speaker's stance or sentiment to the other speakers or to the publisher. Neutral reporting stays neutral; ambiguity stays unclear. The top-level content_reference is an overall frame retained for compatibility: for one viewpoint it mirrors that viewpoint; for a debate it neutrally summarizes the competing positions and uses "Multiple speakers" where appropriate. Never collapse incompatible positions into a single sentiment. Do not guess a viewpoint from comments.

COMMENTS. Classify each supplied comment relative to an identified viewpoint IN THE VIDEO CONTENT, including when the video is neutral. A positive or celebratory reaction can be substantive support when a single proposition is the clear referent; in competing-viewpoint content, require a cue that identifies the selected side. EXECUTION (adapted from Dall’Olio & Vakratsas, Table 5; consumer-advertising categories, not a validated war-content effectiveness scale). Annotate delivery independently of Monitor relevance: related, not_related and unclear videos can all use delivery techniques. Changing relevance to not_related or unclear must never erase, rewrite or invalidate a delivery technique that is independently supported by the supplied evidence. Return exactly FOUR categories: comparative, endorsement, entertainment, mnemonic_devices. Imagery/visual is omitted from this video-only study by researcher choice; do not return it. Each has status, exact transcript evidence quote/source_id when present, and rationale. Use not_applicable only when the delivery category genuinely does not apply to the video; do not use it merely because the subject is outside Monitor scope. Do not blanket-code all four categories not_applicable after a not_related decision; assess each independently. Distinguish this from not_observed_in_transcript (the category could apply but is not evidenced in complete text) and not_observable (available evidence cannot assess it). If transcript_truncated, do not infer absence from omitted material.
comparative: direct OR clearly implied contrast of countries, forces, capabilities, institutions, rights, citizenship/residency rules, daily life or systems, or superiority boasting (puffery). The contrast must be expressed in the supplied text: do not invent a comparator using background knowledge. Two country names alone are insufficient. A question-only quiz about one country is not a comparison without an expressed contrast. A narration contrasting citizenship as a job with citizenship as a birthright qualifies even without naming another country; describing a citizenship rule alone without contrast does not. A security/sovereignty-related video may compare nonmilitary aspects; do not restrict this category to weapons or power. Clear implied contrasts are an explicit adaptation beyond the paper’s direct-comparison definition.
endorsement: identified expert, official, celebrity, or eyewitness/personal testimony used to lend credibility to a claim. A named head of state, official or other person merely appearing as a quiz answer is not testimony or endorsement; a name mentioned in news is not enough; ordinary unnamed narration is not automatically expert endorsement. This describes the delivery device, not agreement by the publisher.
entertainment: explicit humor/comedy, dramatization OR narrated historical storytelling. A narrator recounting past events, protagonists, conflict, actions and outcome counts as storytelling, even when factual, educational or documentary, without requiring jokes or fiction. Historical battle narration (including a narrated victory before Lepanto) is present. A date or isolated historical fact alone is insufficient. This historical-narration rule is an explicit study adaptation.
mnemonic_devices: The paper includes a brand/product name repeated at least three times; our adaptation allows a clearly identified focal country or institution name repeated at least three times in narration. State the repeated name and its observed repetition in the rationale, with an exact quote. Do not equate any memorable description with a mnemonic device. Before marking mnemonic_devices not_observed_in_transcript, scan the supplied caption sections for an actual repeated focal name or slogan and count occurrences; do not rely on a general impression of the narration. Other indicators: distinctive slogan/catchphrase, repeated identifiable name (3+ actual mentions in transcript), memorable character or audio cue. Plain background [Music] captions do not establish a memorable jingle. Verbal repetition can be present when evidenced; otherwise not_observable because sound/visual identity is unobserved.
For comparative, endorsement or entertainment, use not_observed_in_transcript if no verbal evidence in the available complete transcript, NOT a claim of absence from the video. If truncated or ambiguous use not_observable. For present status quotes MUST come from transcript sections, not titles/descriptions. Never assume cinematic editing, imagery, music, charisma, emotional impact or effectiveness from the transcript. Human reviewers will supply audiovisual judgments after watching.

- Scope is substantive, not keyword-only. Country-level unsafe water, finite reserves, flood protection, strategic dependence, national law, citizenship/residency rules, international trade dependencies and political institutions can be related without a military actor. A passing mineral/electronics mention is insufficient unless the evidence connects supply or resources to national/European dependence, resilience, governance, strategy or collective security.

Do not exclude a source merely because religion, culture, humor or personal narrative surrounds the in-scope material. Conversely, a local maintenance grievance is not automatically critical infrastructure or civil protection; require a collective/systemic link.
