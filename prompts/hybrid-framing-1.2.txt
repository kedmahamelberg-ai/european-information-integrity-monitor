comparative: direct OR clearly implied contrast of countries, forces, capabilities, institutions, rights, citizenship/residency rules, daily life or systems, or superiority boasting (puffery). endorsement: identified expert, official, celebrity, or eyewitness/personal testimony used to lend credibility to a claim. A name mentioned in news is not enough; ordinary unnamed narration is not automatically expert endorsement. This describes the delivery device, not agreement by the publisher.
entertainment: explicit humor/comedy, dramatization, or a constructed story with beginning, development, conclusion; routine chronological reporting of events is not automatically storytelling.
