Label definitions and scoring anchors

Othering: social distance, perceived fundamental difference, incomprehension or an us–them boundary. Weak distance can score 1 without hatred. Different policies or physical borders alone do not count.

Aversion: dislike, contempt, hostility, disgust, fear or hatred toward that group.

Moralization: questions the target's integrity or portrays it as dishonest, heartless, corrupt, traitorous or evil. Technical incompetence and service failures alone do not count.

0 absent · 1 weak/implicit · 2 moderate/clear · 3 strong/repeated · 4 extreme/dehumanizing or categorical.

Distinguish reporting and quotation from endorsement. Positive praise or policy criticism alone does not establish sectarian framing.

Score each dimension independently. Insufficient evidence is not zero. This 0–4 coding rubric is an experimental adaptation, not the literature's validated 0–6 survey measure.
