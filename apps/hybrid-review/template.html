Defence readiness 🛡️: preparation, deterrence, defence and resilience. Force projection & coercion ⚔️: displaying power, threats or coercive action. Target of hostile action 🎯: portrayed as attacked, hacked, sabotaged, threatened or coerced. Roles describe portrayals, including attributed allegations.

Comparative: explicit comparison or superiority claims. Endorsement: an expert, official or witness used to lend credibility. Entertainment: humor, dramatization or constructed storytelling. Imagery/visual: striking images, charts, editing or effects. Mnemonic devices: slogans, distinctive audio/characters or repeated names.

These categories were developed for advertising. This study adapts them descriptively; it does not claim that a style causes persuasion, engagement or hybrid warfare.

Comment alignment is agreement with the displayed video proposition. Sentiment is evaluative tone toward its own stated target. Negative sentiment can support a video. No expressed video stance means agreement cannot be assessed.
