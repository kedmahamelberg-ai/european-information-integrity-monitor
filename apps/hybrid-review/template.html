Four execution categories adapt Dall’Olio & Vakratsas (2022), Table 5. Imagery/visual is omitted from this video-only study by researcher choice. Labels use presence/absence (1/0); unavailable evidence is not absence. Narrated historical accounts count as storytelling, including factual documentaries. Background music alone is not a mnemonic device. Comment reviews are optional and recorded separately.

Three questions · worked examples

Example video: Sergio Sayas argues that the government has failed on housing. Speaker: Sayas. Target: government housing policy. Speaker sentiment: negative.

Comment Focus Main-message alignment Sentiment and object
“He laughs at the suffering” (clearly about the criticized PM) Video target Supports the criticism Negative toward PM’s response
“Sergio is a clear, concise speaker” Speaker skill No position on housing claim Positive toward delivery
“Great editing” Presentation No position on housing claim Positive toward editing
“The figures are false; the government is doing well” Main message / target Opposes the criticism Positive toward government performance

If a comment evaluates several things, choose Mixed focus and name the objects. Use Unclear when the referent or irony cannot be established. Do not infer political agreement from emojis alone.

Read the coding guide

Defence readiness 🛡️: preparation, deterrence, defence and resilience. Force projection & coercion ⚔️: displaying power, threats or coercive action. Target of hostile action 🎯: portrayed as attacked, hacked, sabotaged, threatened or coerced. Roles describe portrayals, including attributed allegations.

Comparative: direct or clearly implied contrasts of countries, institutions, citizenship rights, capabilities or everyday life; no invented comparator. Endorsement: an expert, official or witness used to lend credibility. Entertainment: humor, dramatization or narrated historical storytelling, including factual battle accounts. Mnemonic devices: slogans, distinctive audio/characters or repeated names.

The four active categories describe delivery, not persuasion or danger. Historical narration and clear indirect comparisons are explicit study adaptations. Archived reviews retain their original labels.

Topics identify what the video discusses. Country-level water quality/scarcity, energy dependence, food supply and other essential resources are included even without an armed threat. Country comes from title/description; spoken language is preserved separately. A Russian-language report about Moldova is labeled Moldova.

Establish speaker, main message, target and the speaker’s sentiment once per video. For each comment identify its focus, agreement with the main message, and sentiment toward a named object. Criticizing Sánchez can align with a speaker criticizing Sánchez while remaining negative toward Sánchez. Praising the speaker’s delivery or the editing alone gives no position on the main message. A neutral factual report can receive agreement or denial; judging the reported event is not automatically disputing its occurrence. Multiple incompatible speakers or ambiguous targets require uncertainty. National laws and institutions, resource security and international trade agreements are within this study’s broad scope. Topic membership does not establish propaganda or hybrid aggression.
