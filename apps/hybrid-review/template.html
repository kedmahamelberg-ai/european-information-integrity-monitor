Read the coding guide

Defence readiness 🛡️: preparation, deterrence, defence and resilience. Force projection & coercion ⚔️: displaying power, threats or coercive action. Target of hostile action 🎯: portrayed as attacked, hacked, sabotaged, threatened or coerced. Roles describe portrayals, including attributed allegations.

Comparative: direct or clearly implied contrasts of countries, institutions, citizenship rights, capabilities or everyday life; no invented comparator. Endorsement: an expert, official or witness used to lend credibility. Entertainment: humor, dramatization or constructed storytelling. Imagery/visual: striking images, charts, editing or effects. Mnemonic devices: slogans, distinctive audio/characters or repeated names.

Verbal imagery is our separate extension for vivid scene-setting in narration, assessable from the transcript. It does not establish visual production features. The original five categories were developed for advertising. This study adapts them descriptively; it does not claim that a style causes persuasion, engagement or hybrid warfare.

Stance applies only to comments: supports, opposes, mixed, no position, unrelated or unclear in relation to the video content. Name the claim, policy, action or narrative being addressed. A neutral news video can receive supportive or opposing comments. Condemning a reported attack is opposition to that attack, not denial that it happened. Sentiment is the comment’s evaluative tone toward its separately named target; negative sentiment does not automatically mean opposition.
