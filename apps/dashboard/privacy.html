Privacy and advertising

Updated 26 October 2026.

The European Security Monitor is an independent research project created by Kedma Hamelberg, PhD. This notice covers visitors to the public website.

Advertising choices

We use Google AdSense to help fund this project. Ads may appear on the main dashboard and research overview after Google approves the site. Advertising does not determine the research classifications or source selection.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage and identifiers to deliver, personalise and measure ads. Google's consent message explains the purposes and partners where consent is required. Ad requests remain paused until the consent service supplies a permitted state. You can revisit your choice using Advertising privacy choices in the footer of an advertising-supported page.

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and manage personalisation in My Ad Center. Browser cookie controls can also remove stored choices.

Site measurement

The site loads Google Analytics to measure visits and interactions. Page addresses sent by the site's analytics code omit query strings and fragments; Google signals and advertising personalisation are disabled for that measurement. The public visitor counter reports visits, not verified unique people. Advertising consent is separate from these existing site measurements.

Embedded and linked content

Video players and live camera views are supplied by third parties, including YouTube, which may receive technical information when their content loads. External links take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

Research data

The Monitor studies public communications and public audience responses. Its methodology and limitations describe the research separately from advertising and visitor measurement.
