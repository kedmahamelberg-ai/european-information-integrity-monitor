Privacy and advertising

Updated 14 November 2026.

The European Security Monitor is an independent research project created by Kedma Hamelberg, PhD. This notice covers visitors to the public website.

Advertising choices

We use Google AdSense to help fund this project on desktop. Ads may appear on the main dashboard and research overview after Google approves the site. Mobile devices and narrow screens do not load AdSense or its consent message. Advertising does not determine the research classifications or source selection.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage and identifiers to deliver, personalise and measure ads. Google's consent message explains the purposes and partners where consent is required. Ad requests remain paused until the consent service supplies a permitted state. You can revisit your choice using Advertising privacy choices in the footer of an advertising-supported page.

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and manage personalisation in My Ad Center. Browser cookie controls can also remove stored choices.

Site measurement

On phones, tablets and computers, we ask whether you accept analytics cookies before loading Google Analytics or recording a visit in our counter. Accepting enables measurement; rejecting keeps both off. Your choice is stored on this site for up to 180 days. Use Analytics cookie choices in the dashboard footer to change it. Withdrawing permission stops future measurement and clears the Analytics cookies accessible to this site.

When you accept, page addresses sent by our Analytics code omit query strings and fragments; Google signals and advertising personalisation are disabled for that measurement. The visit counter uses a random browser token to avoid recounting a visit within a 30-minute session. Its totals describe consented visits, not verified unique people. Advertising choices are handled separately by Google's consent message.

Embedded and linked content

Video players and live camera views are supplied by third parties, including YouTube, which may receive technical information when their content loads. External links take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

Research data

The Monitor studies public communications and public audience responses. Its methodology and limitations describe the research separately from advertising and visitor measurement.
