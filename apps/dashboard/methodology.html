METHODS & TRANSPARENCY · VERSION 1.0

What the monitor measures

The European Information Integrity Monitor studies how sampled YouTube public-affairs titles and descriptions frame groups, how highly visible retrieved comments relate to that content, and whether comment patterns exhibit amplification anomalies.

Research beta. No live findings yet.
The demonstration is entirely synthetic. Automated measurement is not the validated Political Sectarianism Scale. At least 100 stratified video cases and 20 comment clusters must receive human review before the launch gate permits publication of live analytical results.

01 · Sectarian framing

Finkel and colleagues identify othering, aversion and moralization as components of political sectarianism. Their validated psychological measure uses self-report responses. Our Sectarian Framing Index (SFI) adapts these concepts to text and requires independent validation across languages and contexts.

Dimension Operational definition
Othering A target group is portrayed as alien, incompatible, threatening or outside the legitimate community.
Aversion Contempt, hostility, disgust, fear or hatred directed toward a target group.
Moralization A target group is portrayed as corrupt, dishonest, evil, traitorous or morally illegitimate.

Each dimension is scored 0–4: absent, weak, moderate, strong or extreme. SFI = (Othering + Aversion + Moralization) / 3. A strong sectarian frame requires all three dimensions ≥ 2. Dimension prevalence means a score > 0; continuous scores remain primary. Positive praise does not count. Quoted hostility without endorsement, policy disagreement, and criticism without group delegitimation must be distinguished.

02 · Discovery and sampling

Discovery is video-centred. A versioned multilingual dictionary searches country names, demonyms and variants in titles or descriptions. Generic Europe/EU terms are supplementary, not required. The broad geographic universe includes transcontinental countries, Cyprus and Kosovo; Kosovo is included without a sovereignty determination, using user-assigned XK/XKX codes. Georgia and other ambiguous terms require context. Dictionary coverage is incomplete and must be audited.

The complete retrieved candidate frame is preserved before SFI or narrative outcomes are known. Search is quota-bounded and not a census of YouTube. Each query, returned page, truncation and retrieval time is logged. News relevance uses deterministic checks, multilingual local embeddings and an LLM only for uncertain cases. Five percent of rejected cases are randomly retained for exclusion auditing.

Channels receive a configurable institutionalization score from observable age, subscribers, upload counts, publishing frequency and linked-domain signals. Tiers are empirical tertiles within the discovered frame. Missing fields remain missing; verified status is not inferred. Institutionalization is not trustworthiness.

Sampling uses country × tier strata, with initial 40% low, 40% medium and 20% high benchmark allocations. Multi-country candidates receive one deterministic, outcome-independent sampling-country assignment, while all geographic mentions remain available for analysis. Videos are sampled uniformly without replacement within each stratum. Empty or small strata cause explicit shortfalls rather than hidden replacement. Inclusion probabilities, seed and frame membership are retained. Country summaries are unweighted descriptions of this sample; they are not population prevalence estimates.

03 · One reproducible research clock

Collection runs Sundays after the completed Sunday–Saturday interval in Europe/Amsterdam. The implementation uses a half-open [Sunday 00:00, next Sunday 00:00) interval and stores the inclusive Saturday end for display. Timezone-aware boundaries accommodate 167-, 168- and 169-hour weeks. Collection-Sunday videos are excluded. The batch ID identifies the ISO week containing the closing Saturday. Corrections create new classification versions.

04 · Visible comments, not the whole audience

Content inclusion has no comment threshold. The nested comment sample starts at 20 available comments. The beta retrieves up to 100 top-level comments ordered by YouTube relevance, then retains up to 30 using normalized likes plus replies. These are the most visible comments within the retrieved pool; the API cannot guarantee the global top 30 without exhaustive retrieval. Replies contribute to visibility but are not themselves sampled in this beta. Pool size, pagination limits and retrieval time are retained.

05 · Narratives and displacement

Classification is multi-label, using a versioned taxonomy. OTHER requires a label and explanation. Local multilingual embeddings and coherent clusters identify candidate emerging narratives after three consecutive observed weeks or a configurable volume threshold. A researcher decides whether to promote a label; no automatic taxonomy changes occur.

Narrative injection is the share of analyzed visible comments belonging to coherent off-topic clusters with a substantively different classified narrative from the source. Random unrelated comments do not qualify. Topic similarity, off-topic score, narrative alignment, cluster coherence and denominator remain separately accessible. Similarity thresholds are experimental and require calibration.

06 · Amplification signals

The experimental Amplification Anomaly Index (AAI) combines five components on 0–1 scales: duplicate similarity (25%), visible-comment temporal concentration (20%), cross-video recurrence (20%), topic divergence (20%) and narrative concentration (15%). All weights and thresholds are configurable and the components are inspectable. Time concentration is measured only within retained comments. AAI ≥ 0.70 is the initial high-signal threshold, not a validated decision boundary.

SFI and AAI are separate. Neither measures truthfulness. These observations cannot establish bots, an influence operation, intent, foreign involvement or coordinated action. No overall threat score is produced.

07 · Model use, cost and validation

Rules, language detection, local embeddings, deduplication and clustering precede paid classification. Repeated comments share representative classifications with explicit propagation provenance. Structured responses are range-checked and malformed outputs become errors, never zero scores. Each label preserves model, prompt, taxonomy and classifier versions and raw response separately. A budget reservation precedes every paid request; exhausted work remains pending.

Human QA is stratified across high, low and borderline SFI, confidence, OTHER narratives, high/normal AAI and injection cases, plus random ordinary cases. Human and adjudicated labels never overwrite model labels. The review tool reports dimension agreement, strong-frame agreement, narrative overlap and target overlap from actual reviews. No reliability estimate is claimed until reviews exist.

08 · Storage and limitations

Raw observations, samples, classifications and run accounting use an isolated Supabase schema. Public output contains only explicitly exported, de-identified research data. Weekly snapshots are immutable during their permitted retention period. Research reproducibility does not override API retention or deletion requirements: default raw-data retention is 30 days, and longer archival retention requires a documented permitted basis. Source deletions and raw-data purges are recorded as tombstones.

Key limitations include search ranking and quota truncation, multilingual coverage, title/description-only source measurement, channel metadata missingness, oversampling, highly engaged comment selection, model errors, and observational confounding. Countries identify the subject of content, not the source, nationality, population attitudes or government behaviour. Cross-country counts are not directly comparable without examining sampling and language coverage.
